LABIC / UFES · Academic research

Research begins with understanding data.

Ford is an internal tool for collecting and processing data from digital platforms, supporting research at the Internet and Data Science Lab (LABIC), Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES), Brazil.

Research · Data · Society

01 / About Ford

A tool for research, built for the lab.

Ford supports academic inquiry into digital platforms through internal data collection and analysis.

It is used by authorized researchers and members of LABIC. Ford is an internal, non-commercial tool; it has no public access interface and does not charge for access.

Ford uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API v3, to collect public video and comment data internally. Researchers analyze data stored in a cloud database and local CSV files. This website documents the client; it does not collect data from YouTube.

03 / Institutional context

Research connected to public knowledge.

LABIC is the Internet and Data Science Lab at UFES. Its work connects research on the internet, data and society.

PYSA is a science communication initiative related to the lab’s activities. It is not a public interface for Ford.
