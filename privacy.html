Organization and purpose

The Internet and Data Science Lab (LABIC) at the Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES), Brazil, uses Ford as an internal, non-commercial tool for academic research into digital platforms. Access is restricted to authorized lab members.

YouTube API Services and information collected

Ford uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API v3, to collect public information. This can include video identifiers, titles, descriptions and publication dates; channel identifiers and public information; available view, like and comment counts; and comments, including their text, publication dates and public author identifiers and information. The fields retained depend on the collection and research use. Public author information and comments may contain personal data.

Research use

Researchers use these data to study digital communication, the circulation of information, interactions and social phenomena on digital platforms, and to prepare academic studies and scientific knowledge.

Storage and access

Collected data are stored in a cloud-hosted database that is not publicly accessible and in local CSV files on computers used internally by the lab. Access to the data is restricted to authorized lab members. The existence, location and retention of any backups remain under review.

Sharing and research outputs

Research results may be disclosed in scientific publications and academic communication initiatives, subject to applicable restrictions on API data use and redistribution. Ford has no public interface for querying collected records; public availability of raw YouTube data is not represented by this policy.

Retention, refresh and deletion

The examined collection flows currently have no automatic periodic refresh or deletion routine. Data are deleted manually when requested. Retention periods and the handling of all copies, including possible backups, remain under review. Deleting data held by Ford does not delete it from YouTube; deletion from YouTube must be requested through a YouTube service or a client that supports it.

Privacy and deletion requests

For questions about Ford or its privacy practices, or to request deletion of data held by the lab, contact LABIC through its LABIC contact page. The lab handles deletion requests manually; a specific response time has not yet been confirmed.

External policies and settings

Ford uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy · YouTube Terms of Service · Google Account permissions · YouTube Developer Policies · YouTube API Services Terms of Service. The permissions page lets Google account holders review account permissions; its inclusion does not imply that Ford uses Google sign-in or OAuth.
