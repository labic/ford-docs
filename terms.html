Purpose and access

Ford is an internal, non-commercial academic research tool of LABIC at UFES. It is not sold or offered for a fee. Access is restricted to authorized lab members, and the system has no public access interface.

Responsible use

Authorized operators must use Ford for institutional and academic purposes and observe applicable institutional rules, privacy obligations and YouTube requirements.

YouTube API Services and terms

Ford uses YouTube API Services, including YouTube Data API v3. By using Ford, its authorized operators agree to the YouTube Terms of Service as applicable and must observe the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies applicable to their work.

Use and redistribution of data

YouTube API data must not be used, disclosed or redistributed in ways incompatible with applicable YouTube API terms and policies. Research outputs are subject to these requirements and applicable institutional rules.

Institutional contact

A secure institutional contact for questions about Ford and these terms is not currently available on this site. Ford is not sponsored or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
